### 3. Hosting Requirements
- **Single Page:** Not required (no client-side routing)
- **CSP headers:** Must be applied via `_headers` (Cloudflare/Netlify) or `.htaccess` (Apache)
- **404 page:** Must use `404.html` (auto-detected by most hosts)
- **HTTPS:** Enforce TLS 1.2+ with HSTS header
- **Compression:** Enable Brotli or Gzip for HTML/CSS/JS
- **Cache policy:** 1-year cache for CSS/JS with versioning via filename
